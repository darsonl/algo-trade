## 1. The skipped scan (#67)

**Symptom:** at 21:35 Taipei (09:35 ET) the log reads `Run time of job "... cron[hour='9',
minute='35'] ..." was missed by 0:00:01.397457`, and no stock scan ran. The 22:00 ETF scan ran
normally (VOO, SCHD).

**Root cause:** a clock step met APScheduler's one-second misfire default.
1. The task woke the PC at 20:00. The scheduler started at 20:00:16 and computed its wait for 21:35.
2. At **20:00:19**, the Windows time service stepped the clock forward **+1.363 s**
   (`12:00:18.058 → 12:00:19.421 UTC`; System log, Kernel-General event 1, right after a
   Time-Service sync with time.windows.com). The clock had drifted while the host slept.
3. The wait is **monotonic**, so it did not move with the step. The job woke at a wall-clock
   21:35:01.397, which is 1.363 s plus the usual ~35 ms.
4. Scan jobs had no `misfire_grace_time`, so the **1-second default** discarded the scan as a misfire.

**Why only a clock step does this:** over the 33 scans logged before it, the worst start latency was
**44 ms**. The wake-then-sync sequence repeats on any evening the drift exceeds a second, so this
would recur.

**Fix:** `main.SCAN_MISFIRE_GRACE_S = 600`, set on every scan job in `configure_scheduler`.
- **Finite on purpose.** A scan slept through for hours must still be MISSED, because the
  post-scan exit listener arms on it and its prices would be stale. The old docstring said
  "scans keep the 1-second default on purpose" without a reason. The reason was this, and one
  second over-delivered on it.
- **Ten minutes** keeps a late 09:35 ET stock scan clear of the 10:00 ET ETF scan. A colliding scan
  is SKIPPED by the shared scan lock.
- **Tests:** 3 new in `test_sleep_and_reconnect.py`, through a real `BackgroundScheduler` with the
  job's next run time moved into the past.
  - A scan 1.4 s late **runs**. This reproduces the incident and was red before the fix.
  - A scan 3 h late is **MISSED**.
  - The grace is bounded.
  - Mutants were checked in both directions: `None` fails the hours test, `1` fails the 1.4 s test.
- CLAUDE.md records the decision (post-scan exit section) and the new count. **1643 tests**; CI green
  on 3.11 and 3.12.

**Recovery that night:** the operator ran `/scan` at 22:13, inside the 30-min approval window. It
completed at 22:21 with **8 recommendations**.

**What to watch:** the first 20:00 start after a long sleep should show the scan's `Running job` line
within a second or two of 21:35, even when the System log shows a time step. A `was missed by` line
on a scan is now worth investigating, since only a gap over 10 minutes produces one.
