<objective>
Wire the limit buy infrastructure (from Plan 01) into the Discord approval flow and the scan loop: (a) add scan_time kwarg to build_recommendation_embed so the Price field shows "as of HH:MM" staleness, (b) route ApproveRejectView.approve to place_limit_order or place_order based on config.use_limit_buy, (c) pass limit_price/order_type to create_trade for audit trail, (d) capture scan_time in main.py before the ticker loop and forward through send_recommendation, and (e) write 9 tests covering the routing, create_trade kwargs, confirmation messages, and embed Price field.

Note: ETF approvals also route through ApproveRejectView.approve, so use_limit_buy=True will place limit orders for ETF buys too. This is intentional — limit orders are strictly safer than market orders regardless of asset type. The ETF scan embed and prompt paths are not modified (per scope in 17-CONTEXT.md).
